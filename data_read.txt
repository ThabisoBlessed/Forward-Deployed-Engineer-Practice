Those four sections tell a complete story:
1. Data Quality – Can management trust the data? What duplicates, missing values or inconsistencies exist?
2. Business KPIs – How is the business performing? Revenue, orders, cancellation rate, delivery performance, etc.
3. Trends and Anomalies – What's changing over time? Which categories are growing or declining? Are there unusual patterns?
4. Process Inefficiency – Where is the business losing time, money or operational efficiency? Late deliveries, delays, bottlenecks, etc.


For every major finding, aim for:

Finding → Evidence → Business Impact → Recommendation

For example:
- Finding: Cancellation rate increased in September.
- Evidence: Cancellation rate rose from 4% to 9%.
- Business impact: More orders are being lost before fulfilment.
- Recommendation: Investigate cancellation reasons and identify whether specific categories or regions are responsible.
