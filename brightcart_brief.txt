BRIGHTCART — TECHNICAL ASSESSMENT (practice)

Business context: BrightCart is an online electronics retailer operating
across South Africa. Management wants an independent analysis of the
last 12 months of trading (Nov 2025 - Oct 2026) to answer three questions:
Are we growing? Where are we losing money? Which clients/areas need
attention? You will connect to a database, analyse the data, and deliver
a report presenting your findings.

You have 3 hours. Structure your time:
  17:00-17:20  understand the schema, profile tables, data-quality audit
  17:20-18:50  analysis
  18:50-19:50  write the report

Deliverable: an Executive Summary, Key Findings (max 4, each with a chart
and a "so what"), Recommendations (max 3), Caveats.

TASKS
1. Data-quality audit: duplicates, NULLs, inconsistent values. State how
   each issue is handled.
2. Monthly revenue trend for the last 12 months. State which order
   statuses count as revenue.
3. Category analysis: revenue and refund rate per category
   (refund rate = refunded orders / all orders in that category).
   Which categories lose the most money to refunds?
4. Delivery vs cancelled orders: is there a pattern between how long an
   order stayed in "shipped" status and cancellations? (Hint: use
   payments.pay_date or order date - keep it simple, or state why this
   can't be answered with the data available - that is also a valid finding.)
5. At-risk revenue: orders with no matching payment record, or paid more
   than 30 days after order date. Total amount and top 5 customers.
6. Write the report per the structure above.

RULES: solo work. State all assumptions. Any number in the report must
be traceable to a query. Use SQLite-compatible SQL.
